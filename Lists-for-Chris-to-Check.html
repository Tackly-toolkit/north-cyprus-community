Areas and categories members will pick from

Members choose from these lists instead of typing, so the names must be right. Please check every spelling, every alternative name, and which town sits in which district. Add anything missing, and cross out anything wrong.

How to reply: a quick list of changes is enough, for example "add Karşıyaka under Girne" or "Alsancak is also called Karavas".

Where: district, then town, then area

The member picks a district, then a town, then an area. Alternative names (shown in brackets) are searchable, so someone typing Kyrenia finds Girne.
